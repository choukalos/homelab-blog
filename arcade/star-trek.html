THE MISSION

You command the Enterprise in a sector of Klingon space. Maneuver with the sticks and fire your phasers — but the phaser charge is limited and only recharges over time, so don't waste shots. Blast the Klingon fighters and cruisers, dodge their return fire, and destroy the Klingon base to warp to the next sector.
