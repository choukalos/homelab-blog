THE FIGHT

Work your way right through four stages — Street, Alley, Rooftop, Dojo — punching and kicking the thugs, cops, and bikers in your path. Fill the energy meter to unleash the DOUBLE DRAGON combo, then take down the boss in the dojo to clear the round.

CONTROLS

UP / W Jump

X / J Punch (fast, light)

C / K Kick (slow, heavy knockback)

V / L Double Dragon (special — needs a full meter)

P Pause
